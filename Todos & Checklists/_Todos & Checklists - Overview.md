# Todos & Checklists — Overview

**Todos** are simple tracked tasks — a name, a status, a priority, an optional due date — used throughout OCU One wherever something needs doing. A todo can stand alone, live in the shared Todos area, or be attached directly to a job, project, or other record. **Checklists** are a related feature that let you break a single todo down into a list of smaller steps.

## The seven guides

**Creating, editing, and managing a standalone todo** covers the main Todos area — the list, creating a todo, editing it, and deleting one. See [[Creating, editing, and managing a standalone todo]].

**Changing a todo's status from its detail page** covers the quick status dropdown on a todo's own page. See [[Changing a todo's status from its detail page]].

**Viewing todos as a pipeline (kanban board)** covers the alternative board view of the same Todos area, grouped by status into columns. See [[Viewing todos as a pipeline (kanban board)]].

**Adding and managing todos on a job or project (Todos tab)** covers attaching todos directly to a job or project via its own Todos tab, rather than the standalone list. See [[Adding and managing todos on a job or project (Todos tab)]].

**Viewing and prioritising your assigned todos** covers the Todos tab of My Assignments — your personal view of everything assigned to you, and how to flag a todo as a priority. See [[Viewing and prioritising your assigned todos]].

**Adding a checklist to a todo** and **Adding, checking off, and removing checklist items** cover checklists — a feature that lives on individual todos, letting you break one down into a set of smaller steps that can each be ticked off. See [[Adding a checklist to a todo]] and [[Adding, checking off, and removing checklist items]].

## Why this matters

Todos are the app's general-purpose way of tracking "things that need doing" — separate from the more structured workflows around jobs, projects, and tickets. Whether it's a personal reminder, a task assigned to someone else, or a small step within a larger piece of work, todos give you one consistent way to track it, see its status, and know when it's due — and checklists extend that down to the individual-step level when a single todo needs more than a one-line description to capture what's involved.
