# Browsing Defects

Defects raised against any permit can all be seen together in one table, rather than having to open each permit individually to check its Defects tab.

## Getting there

From the [Permits landing page](Using%20the%20permits%20landing%20page.md), click the **Defects** card. You can also reach this list directly from the sidebar.

## What you'll see

A table listing every defect with its **Title**, **Reference**, **Status** (New, Acknowledged, Disputed, or Closed), **Defect Type** (New, Old, 2 hours, 4 hours, or Reissued), and **Inspection Date**:

![Defects table showing five defects with varying statuses and defect types: Footway ramp uneven (Disputed, Old), Missing warning lights (Closed, New), Signage missing (New, Old), TM signage faded (New, Reissued), and Trench left unbacked overnight (Acknowledged, 2 hours)](attachments/browsing-defects/01-defects-list.jpg)

## Filtering and sorting

Use the filter chips along the top — **Include closed?**, **Title**, **Reference**, **Status**, and **Defect Type** — or click **+ Filter** for more options. Click any column header's arrows to sort.

## Opening a defect

Click a defect's title to open its overview — see [Viewing a defect](Viewing%20a%20defect.md). Each defect belongs to one permit, shown in the breadcrumb at the top of its page, so from there you can also jump back to that permit.
