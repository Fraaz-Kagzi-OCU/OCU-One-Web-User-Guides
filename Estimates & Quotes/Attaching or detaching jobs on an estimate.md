# Attaching or detaching jobs on an estimate

An estimate's **Jobs** tab links it to the actual jobs that carry out the work it scopes out.

## Viewing linked jobs

Open an estimate and click its **Jobs** tab. If nothing is linked yet, you'll see an empty state and an **+ Attach** button.

![An empty Jobs tab on an estimate, with a "+ Attach" button](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/01-jobs-tab-empty.jpg)

## Attaching a job

Click **+ Attach** and choose:

![The Attach dropdown open, showing "New" and "Existing" options](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/02-attach-dropdown.jpg)

- **New** — create a brand new job, pre-linked to this estimate.
- **Existing** — link a job that already exists.

Choosing **Existing** opens a search field — type the job's title and pick it from the results.

![Searching for "Turbine" in the job picker, showing "Turbine Blade Inspection Visit" as a match](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/03-search-job.jpg)

Click the link icon next to the field to confirm. A "Job was successfully attached" notice appears, and the job now shows in the table with its client, job type, permit status, booked date, allocated user, priority, and current status.

![The Jobs tab showing "Turbine Blade Inspection Visit" attached, with client, job type, priority, and status columns](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/04-job-attached.jpg)

## Detaching a job

Scroll the table right to reveal the trash icon in the **Status** column, and click it.

![The Jobs table scrolled right, showing the Status column and a trash/detach icon next to the attached job](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/05-job-attached-scrolled-detach-icon.jpg)

A confirmation dialog appears — click **Yes** to detach it, or **Cancel** to leave it linked.

![The "Are you sure you want to detach this?" confirmation dialog with Cancel and Yes buttons](attachments/attaching-or-detaching-jobs-on-an-estimate/06-detach-confirm.jpg)

Detaching a job doesn't delete it — it just removes the link between the job and this estimate.
