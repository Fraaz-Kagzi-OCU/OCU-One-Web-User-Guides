# Editing, activating/deactivating, or deleting an estimate

Draft estimates — Unsent, Planned, or Ready — can be edited or deleted freely. Once Pending or further along, they're locked from changes (see [Viewing an estimate overview and updating status/RAG](Viewing%20an%20estimate%20overview%20and%20updating%20status%20RAG.md)).

## Editing

Open an estimate and click **Edit** in the header.

![An estimate's overview page in Planned status, with Edit and delete buttons available](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/01-overview-planned.jpg)

Change any field — Title, Rate Book, Version, Description, Reference, Files — the same form used when creating it.

![The edit form with the Title field changed to "Draft Turbine Site Survey Estimate (Revised)"](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/02-edit-title.jpg)

Click **Update Estimate** to save. The change appears immediately, including in the page title.

![The estimate's title now reading "Draft Turbine Site Survey Estimate (Revised)"](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/03-title-updated.jpg)

## Activating and deactivating

Click the eye icon in the header to deactivate an estimate — a confirmation dialog appears first.

![The "Are you sure you want to deactivate this?" confirmation dialog](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/04-deactivate-confirm.jpg)

Click **Yes** to confirm. The icon changes to a crossed-out eye, and the Activity feed records who deactivated it and when. Deactivating doesn't change the estimate's status (it stayed **Planned** here) — it's a separate lifecycle flag from the status workflow.

![The estimate now deactivated, shown by the crossed-out eye icon and an Activity entry "Marcus Webb deactivated Estimate"](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/05-deactivated.jpg)

Click the crossed-out eye icon again to reactivate it, confirming the same way.

![The "Are you sure you want to activate this?" confirmation dialog](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/06-activate-confirm.jpg)

![The estimate reactivated, shown by the Activity entry "Marcus Webb activated Estimate"](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/07-activated.jpg)

## Deleting

Click the trash icon in the header.

![The "Are you sure you want to delete this?" confirmation dialog](attachments/editing-activating-deactivating-or-deleting-an-estimate/08-delete-confirm.jpg)

Click **Yes** to permanently delete the estimate — it's removed from the Estimates list immediately and can't be recovered. Deleting is only available while an estimate is unlocked; a Pending or later-stage estimate has no delete option.
