# Estimates & Quotes — Overview

An **estimate** scopes out proposed work and its likely cost for a project, and can be turned into a **quote** — a PDF you generate for your own records or send to a client for approval.

- [Viewing and filtering estimates and the estimates pipeline](Viewing%20and%20filtering%20estimates%20and%20the%20estimates%20pipeline.md) — the estimates list as a table or a status pipeline board

- [Viewing an estimate overview and updating status/RAG](Viewing%20an%20estimate%20overview%20and%20updating%20status%20RAG.md)
- [Editing, activating/deactivating, or deleting an estimate](Editing%2C%20activating%20deactivating%2C%20or%20deleting%20an%20estimate.md)

- [Attaching or detaching jobs on an estimate](Attaching%20or%20detaching%20jobs%20on%20an%20estimate.md)
- [Viewing records attached to an estimate](Viewing%20records%20attached%20to%20an%20estimate.md)
- [Creating and sending a quote (PDF) to a client](Creating%20and%20sending%20a%20quote%20%28PDF%29%20to%20a%20client.md)

An estimate moves through a status workflow — Unsent → Planned/Pending → Ready → Approved → Won (or Rejected/Internally Rejected/Archived along the way) — and only unlocks for editing while it's Unsent, Planned, or Ready.
