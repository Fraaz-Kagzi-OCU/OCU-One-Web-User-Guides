# Browsing upcoming jobs on the Coming Up widget

The **Coming Up** widget lists your next few scheduled jobs, soonest first.

![Coming Up widget listing two jobs with how soon they start, their title, and job type](attachments/browsing-upcoming-jobs-on-the-coming-up-widget/01-coming-up-widget.jpg)

Each row shows:

- How soon the job starts (e.g. "about 3 hours", "2 days")
- The job's title, which links straight to the job
- The job's site (shown as "None" if no site is set)
- The job type

Only jobs that are Ready for Scheduling, Booked, or In Progress — and due to start soon — appear here. Once a job's start time passes, it drops off this list.
