# Filtering the Projects by Type chart

The **Top Projects by Type** chart shows the split of your projects by project type, as a pie chart, over a period.

1. Click the time period in the top right (defaults to **This Month**).

   ![Top Projects by Type chart with the time period dropdown highlighted](attachments/filtering-the-projects-by-type-chart/01-top-projects-by-type-widget-annotated.jpg)

2. Choose a different period from the list.

   ![Time period options: Previous Month, Previous 7 Days, Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, This Week, This Month, Next 7 Days, Next Month](attachments/filtering-the-projects-by-type-chart/02-time-range-options.jpg)

Unlike the stage chart, this one goes by when the project was created, not by any scheduled dates.
