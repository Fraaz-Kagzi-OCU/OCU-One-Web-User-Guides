# Filtering the Jobs by Type chart

The **Top Jobs by Type** chart shows the split of your jobs by job type, as a pie chart, over a period.

1. Click the time period in the top right (defaults to **This Month**).

   ![Top Jobs by Type chart with the time period dropdown highlighted](attachments/filtering-the-jobs-by-type-chart/01-top-jobs-by-type-widget-annotated.jpg)

2. Choose a different period from the list.

   ![Time period options: Previous Month, Previous 7 Days, Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow, This Week, This Month, Next 7 Days, Next Month](attachments/filtering-the-jobs-by-type-chart/02-time-range-options.jpg)

The pie chart updates to show the proportion of each job type for that period — hover over a slice to see its name and count.
