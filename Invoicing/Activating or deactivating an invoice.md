# Activating or deactivating an invoice

This is only available while an invoice is still **Unsent**.

1. Open the invoice's Overview page.
2. Click the eye icon next to Edit, near the top right.

   ![The invoice's action icons — eye (activate/deactivate) and bin (delete)](attachments/activating-or-deactivating-an-invoice/01-invoice-overview-active.jpg)

3. Confirm the action when asked.

   ![Confirming deactivation](attachments/activating-or-deactivating-an-invoice/02-deactivate-confirm.jpg)

Once deactivated, the eye icon shows with a line through it.

![The eye icon showing the invoice is now deactivated](attachments/activating-or-deactivating-an-invoice/03-deactivated-icon.jpg)

Click the same icon again and confirm to reactivate — it returns to a plain
eye icon.

![Confirming reactivation](attachments/activating-or-deactivating-an-invoice/04-activate-confirm.jpg)
