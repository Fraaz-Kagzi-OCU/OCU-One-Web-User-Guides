# Invoicing — Overview

An **invoice** is created against a project to bill a client for work
completed, and moves through a status pipeline (Unsent → Pending → Approved
→ Paid, or Void/Disputed/Archived along the way). See [Creating a new invoice](Creating%20a%20new%20invoice.md)
for starting one, [Managing invoice lines](Managing%20invoice%20lines.md) for
pulling in recorded product usage to bill, [Updating invoice status and RAG rating](Updating%20invoice%20status%20and%20RAG%20rating.md)
for moving it through its lifecycle, [Creating and sending an invoice bill (PDF) to a client](Creating%20and%20sending%20an%20invoice%20bill%20%28PDF%29%20to%20a%20client.md)
for turning it into a PDF a client receives, [Editing or deleting an invoice](Editing%20or%20deleting%20an%20invoice.md)
and [Activating or deactivating an invoice](Activating%20or%20deactivating%20an%20invoice.md)
for managing it while it's still Unsent, [Viewing an invoice overview](Viewing%20an%20invoice%20overview.md)
for its detail page, [Viewing and filtering the invoices list and pipeline](Viewing%20and%20filtering%20the%20invoices%20list%20and%20pipeline.md)
for browsing them all, and [Viewing an invoice's export history](Viewing%20an%20invoice%27s%20export%20history.md)
for seeing which exports a bill has been included in.
