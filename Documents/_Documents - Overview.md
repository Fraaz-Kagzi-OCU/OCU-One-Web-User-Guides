# Documents — Overview

**Docs** is the file-attachment area found on most records in OCU One — jobs, projects, tickets, and more. You can upload files directly, organise them into folders, preview and annotate them, keep a version history when a file gets replaced, and discuss a document with colleagues via its comments.

## The five guides

**Uploading and organising documents on a record** covers the Docs area itself — uploading a file with or without a folder, and creating a folder to keep related documents together. See [[Uploading and organising documents on a record]].

**Previewing, replacing, and annotating a document** covers everything you can do once a document exists — a quick inline preview, adding a short note, and swapping in an updated copy while keeping its history. See [[Previewing, replacing, and annotating a document]].

**Viewing a document's version history** covers looking back through every past copy of a document that's ever been uploaded to replace an earlier one. See [[Viewing a document's version history]].

**Commenting on a document** covers the comment thread on a document's own page — labelled "Notes" on screen, but the same discussion feature used elsewhere in the app. See [[Commenting on a document]].

**Creating and configuring a folder** covers a folder's full settings in detail — warning when empty, auto-expiry, colour, and more. See [[Creating and configuring a folder]].

## Why this matters

Field-services and back-office work generates a lot of paperwork — certificates, risk assessments, method statements, site photos — and keeping it attached to the right job or project, easy to find, and up to date matters both for day-to-day work and for compliance. Docs gives every record the same consistent way to hold that paperwork: folders for structure, expiry dates for anything time-limited, version history so nothing is lost when a document is corrected, and comments so a question about a specific file doesn't need a separate conversation somewhere else.
