# Search & Navigation — Overview

**Search & Navigation** covers the two shortcuts built into every page for getting where you need to go quickly — without clicking through menus. They're unrelated to each other beyond that shared purpose, so there's no real sequence between them: use whichever one fits what you're trying to find.

## Finding something inside OCU One

The **Search** bar at the top of every page is for finding things that already exist in the app — a project, job, record, ticket, user, or almost anything else. See [Using global search / command palette](Using%20global%20search%20-%20command%20palette.md) for how to narrow a search to a specific type of thing, what the results look like, and when to switch on **Deep Search** for cases where the normal search comes up empty.

## Jumping out to a connected app

The **App Launcher** (the grid icon next to the notification bell) is for jumping to *other* tools your organisation has connected to OCU One — a reporting dashboard, a fleet tracking system, and so on — rather than anything stored inside OCU One itself. See [Launching connected apps from the App Launcher](Launching%20connected%20apps%20from%20the%20App%20Launcher.md) for how to open it, search or arrow-key through a long list of apps, and launch one in a new tab.
