# Hub - Channels — Overview

**Channels** are topic-based feeds in the Hub where a team or subject area shares posts, articles, and surveys. See [Browsing Hub channels and viewing a channel's feed](Browsing%20Hub%20channels%20and%20viewing%20a%20channel%27s%20feed.md) for finding your way around, [Creating and configuring a Hub channel](Creating%20and%20configuring%20a%20Hub%20channel.md) for setting one up, [Writing and publishing a quick post in a channel](Writing%20and%20publishing%20a%20quick%20post%20in%20a%20channel.md) and [Writing and publishing a full article in a channel](Writing%20and%20publishing%20a%20full%20article%20in%20a%20channel.md) for sharing content through its draft → review → publish workflow, and [Acknowledging (marking as read) a post or article](Acknowledging%20%28marking%20as%20read%29%20a%20post%20or%20article.md) for how readers confirm they've seen something marked Must Read. Surveys sent through a channel have their own topic — see *Hub - Surveys*.
