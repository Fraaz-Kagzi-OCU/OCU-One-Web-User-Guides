# Scheduling — Overview

The **Scheduler** is the drag-and-drop board where dispatchers assign jobs to field users on a calendar, one row per bookable person. Jobs wait in a **Ready for Scheduling** panel until they're dragged onto a user's row and booked, or created directly on the board. See:

- [Using the scheduling board (navigation, zoom, time span, user search)](Using%20the%20scheduling%20board.md) — finding your way around the board itself.
- [Booking a job on the scheduler](Booking%20a%20job%20on%20the%20scheduler.md) — the drag-then-confirm flow for scheduling a waiting job.
- [Creating a job directly on the scheduler](Creating%20a%20job%20directly%20on%20the%20scheduler.md) — drag-creating a brand-new job on an empty slot.
- [Unbooking a job from the scheduler](Unbooking%20a%20job%20from%20the%20scheduler.md) — taking a booked job back to "Ready for Scheduling" without losing its slot.
- [Unallocating a job from a user on the scheduler](Unallocating%20a%20job%20from%20a%20user%20on%20the%20scheduler.md) — removing a job from a user's row entirely.
- [Editing a job from the scheduler](Editing%20a%20job%20from%20the%20scheduler.md) — updating a job's details without leaving the board.
- [Bulk-booking many jobs](Bulk-booking%20many%20jobs.md) — confirming several tentatively-placed jobs at once.
