# Responding to a survey

Once a survey has been published to a channel you can see, its card in the feed becomes clickable. Clicking it opens the actual questions for you to fill in.

## Filling in your response

Click the survey's card in the channel feed to open its questions, grouped under the section headings set up by whoever built the survey type.

![Blank survey response form titled "Q3 Site Safety Feedback" with a "Site Conditions" section containing Weather, Overall rating, Hazards, and PPE questions](attachments/Responding%20to%20a%20survey/01-blank-response-form.jpg)

Answer each question — dropdowns for single-choice questions, a free-text box for open-ended ones — then click **Complete Survey**.

![Same form fully filled in: Weather = Yes, Overall site safety rating = Good, Hazards observed with a written note, PPE worn correctly = Yes](attachments/Responding%20to%20a%20survey/02-filled-response-form.jpg)

## After submitting

You're taken back to the channel, with a "Survey was successfully created" confirmation. The survey's card now shows a green checkmark next to your name, confirming you've already responded.

![Channel feed showing the survey card with a green checkmark indicating a completed response](attachments/Responding%20to%20a%20survey/03-submitted-confirmation.jpg)

## Things to know

- You can only respond to a survey once — trying to open it again after completing it takes you to a read-only view of your own answers instead of a blank form.
- A survey's card only ever shows as clickable once it's actually **Published** — while it's still Draft, In Review, or Ready to Publish, there's nothing for you to click yet.
