# Hub - Surveys — Overview

A **survey** is a set of questions sent out through a Hub channel, built from a **Survey Type** configured in advance under Settings (see [Configuring Hub survey types](../Settings/Hub%20Administration/Configuring%20Hub%20survey%20types.md)). See [Creating a survey (announcement) and setting up its questions](Creating%20a%20survey%20%28announcement%29%20and%20setting%20up%20its%20questions.md) for starting one, [Submitting, approving, and publishing a survey request](Submitting%2C%20approving%2C%20and%20publishing%20a%20survey%20request.md) for taking it from Draft through to Published, [Responding to a survey](Responding%20to%20a%20survey.md) for how people fill it in once it's live, and [Viewing survey results and individual responses](Viewing%20survey%20results%20and%20individual%20responses.md) for the aggregated results dashboard.
