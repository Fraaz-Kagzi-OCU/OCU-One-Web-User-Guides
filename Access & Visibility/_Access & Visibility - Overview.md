# Access & Visibility — Overview

Every record in the app — a Job, Ticket, Project, Record, and so on — has an **Access panel**, found in the sidebar of its Main (Overview) tab. It controls who can see the record (**Visibility**), who owns it (**Ownership**), who else it's shared with, who it's assigned to, and how it's tagged or labelled.

This folder covers the Access panel itself and everything you can do inside it:

- [Viewing a record's Access panel (visibility & ownership)](Viewing%20a%20record's%20Access%20panel%20%28visibility%20%26%20ownership%29.md) — opening the panel, and setting who can see a record and who owns it.
- [Sharing or assigning a record to specific users](Sharing%20or%20assigning%20a%20record%20to%20specific%20users.md) — giving named people access, separately from who's responsible for the record.
- [Tagging or labelling a record from the Access panel](Tagging%20or%20labelling%20a%20record%20from%20the%20Access%20panel.md) — categorising a record with tags and labels.
