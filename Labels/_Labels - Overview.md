# Labels — Overview

**Labels** are small coloured tags you can attach to records — like tickets, jobs, or projects — as your own personal way of marking them, on top of whatever statuses or pipelines your organisation already uses. You manage the ones you own from **My Labels**, reached from your profile menu.

## The three pieces

**Managing your personal labels ("My Labels")** covers the page itself — what it shows, how active and inactive labels are separated, and what each row's controls do. See [[Managing your personal labels (My Labels)]].

**Creating a new label** walks through the "+ Label" form — title, colour, description, reference, and optionally assigning the label to a label group. See [[Creating a new label]].

**Editing, deactivating, or deleting a label** covers changing a label's details, taking it out of use with the toggle (and bringing it back), and what "deleting" actually means for a label today. See [[Editing, deactivating, or deleting a label]].

## Why this matters

Labels give you a personal layer of organisation that sits on top of the app's built-in workflow — useful for marking things that need your attention in a way that's meaningful to you, without changing anything about the record's actual status. Because a label you create is yours (only you see it on My Labels, though others can see it applied to a record if you share it), it's a lightweight way to track what matters to you personally across everything you work on.
