# Changing a visit's status

Every visit carries a **Status** — Pending, Planned, Failed, or Done —
shown on its Overview tab.

## Where to find it

On a visit's **Overview** tab, the Status field sits at the top right,
shown as a plain coloured label.

![The Status field on a visit's overview page, showing "Pending" as a static label](attachments/changing-a-visits-status/01-status-field-static.jpg)

Clicking the label does nothing — it isn't a button or dropdown, just a
badge reflecting the visit's current status. The visit's **Edit** page
doesn't have a Status field either.

## How status actually changes

A visit's status updates on its own in one situation: attaching it to a
job automatically moves it from Pending to Planned (and detaching moves it
back to Pending) — see
[Attaching or detaching a visit from a job](Attaching%20or%20detaching%20a%20visit%20from%20a%20job.md).
Beyond that, there's currently no way to set a visit to Failed or Done, or
to move it back to Pending manually, from anywhere in the app.
