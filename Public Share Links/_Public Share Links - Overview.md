# Public Share Links — Overview

**Public Share Links** let a Project Type or Record Type accept new Projects or Records from people who aren't logged into OCU One at all — for example, a member of the public reporting a fault through a link on your website. See [Enabling a Public Intake Link for a Project Type or Record Type](Enabling%20a%20Public%20Intake%20Link%20for%20a%20Project%20Type%20or%20Record%20Type.md) for turning the feature on, [Accessing a Shared Intake Link and Identifying Yourself](Accessing%20a%20Shared%20Intake%20Link%20and%20Identifying%20Yourself.md) for what a visitor sees when they open the link, [Submitting a New Project Through a Public Share Link](Submitting%20a%20New%20Project%20Through%20a%20Public%20Share%20Link.md) and [Submitting a New Record Through a Public Share Link](Submitting%20a%20New%20Record%20Through%20a%20Public%20Share%20Link.md) for the actual submission forms.
