# Hub - Pages — Overview

**Pages** are OCU One's built-in intranet — reference material like handbooks and policies, organized into a nested tree so a top-level page (e.g. a Company Handbook) can hold related pages underneath it. See [Creating and organizing Hub pages (intranet CMS pages)](Creating%20and%20organizing%20Hub%20pages%20%28intranet%20CMS%20pages%29.md) for setting one up, [Editing a Hub page's hero image, featured media, and settings](Editing%20a%20Hub%20page%27s%20hero%20image%2C%20featured%20media%2C%20and%20settings.md) and [Writing and updating a Hub page's body content inline](Writing%20and%20updating%20a%20Hub%20page%27s%20body%20content%20inline.md) for filling one in, [Submitting, approving, rejecting, and publishing a Hub page](Submitting%2C%20approving%2C%20rejecting%2C%20and%20publishing%20a%20Hub%20page.md) for its review workflow, and [Accepting a Hub page (policy/document acknowledgment)](Accepting%20a%20Hub%20page.md) for how readers formally acknowledge a policy page.
