# Client Portal — Overview

The **Client Portal** is the one part of OCU One an external client can reach without an account — a link emailed to them takes them straight to a specific quote to approve or reject, with no sign-in required. See [Approving or rejecting a quote via a client portal link](Approving%20or%20rejecting%20a%20quote%20via%20a%20client%20portal%20link.md) for the full walkthrough.
