# Assignments — Overview

Your **Assignments inbox** is a personal view of everything that's been assigned specifically to you, across the app, gathered into one place rather than scattered across separate lists. It has four tabs — **Jobs**, **Todos**, **Records**, and **Projects** — each showing the "assigned to me" view for that type of record. See [Viewing my assigned jobs](Viewing%20my%20assigned%20jobs.md) for the Jobs tab; the Todos, Records, and Projects tabs work the same way and aren't written up yet.
