# Records — Overview

**Records** cover the wide range of forms, checks, and reports your organisation keeps — from safety inspections to vehicle checks — organised into categories called **Record Groups** so you can browse by the area you actually work in. See [Browsing records by category (Record Groups hub)](Browsing%20records%20by%20category%20%28Record%20Groups%20hub%29.md) for the entry-point hub. This is the largest still-unwritten area of the whole project (25 more guides on creating, viewing, and managing individual records aren't written yet) — this overview will grow as those land.
