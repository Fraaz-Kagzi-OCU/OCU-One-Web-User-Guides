# Records — Overview

**Records** cover the wide range of forms, checks, and reports your organisation keeps — from safety inspections to vehicle checks — organised into categories called **Record Groups** so you can browse by the area you actually work in. See [Browsing records by category (Record Groups hub)](Browsing%20records%20by%20category%20%28Record%20Groups%20hub%29.md) and [Browsing all record type categories (Record Groups landing page)](Browsing%20all%20record%20type%20categories%20%28Record%20Groups%20landing%20page%29.md) for the two entry points into records.

From there, guides cover the full lifecycle of a single record: [choosing its type](Choosing%20a%20record%20type%20before%20creating%20a%20new%20record.md) and [creating it](Creating%20a%20new%20record.md); [viewing its Overview tab](Viewing%20a%20record's%20Overview-Main%20tab.md), [editing](Editing%20a%20record's%20details.md), [deleting](Deleting%20%28archiving%29%20a%20record.md), and [setting its RAG status](Setting%20a%20record's%20RAG%20status.md); [managing its todos](Managing%20todos%20on%20a%20record.md); linking it to [assets](Managing%20assets%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [jobs](Managing%20jobs%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [issues](Managing%20issues%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [estimates](Managing%20estimates%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [clients](Managing%20clients%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [projects](Managing%20projects%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [variations](Managing%20variations%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [invoices](Managing%20invoices%20linked%20to%20a%20record.md), [other records](Managing%20linked%20records%20%28record-to-record%20relationships%29.md), [child records of a specific type](Managing%20child%20records%20of%20a%20specific%20type%20%28Records%20tab%29.md), and [permits](Attaching%20and%20detaching%20a%20permit%20on%20a%20record.md); [downloading it as PDF/Word](Downloading%20a%20record%20as%20PDF%20or%20Word%20document.md); and [moving it through a pipeline](Changing%20and%20reordering%20a%20record's%20pipeline%20stage.md), including the [table](Viewing%20the%20Records%20list-table%20view.md), [filtered](Filtering%20and%20searching%20records%20in%20the%20list%20view.md), and [Kanban board](Viewing%20and%20filtering%20the%20Records%20pipeline-kanban%20board.md) views.
