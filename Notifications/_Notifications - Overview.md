# Notifications — Overview

**Notifications** are how the app tells you something needs your attention without you having to go looking for it — you're booked onto a job, a job you were on gets unbooked, your shift ends, someone mentions you in a comment, and more. Rather than being scattered across the app, every one of these lands in one place: a bell icon in the top right of any page, with a red dot whenever something's unread.

Opening the bell shows your notifications split into **Unread** and **Read**, each showing what happened and how long ago. Clicking a notification takes you straight to whatever it's about — the job, the timesheet, whatever triggered it — and marks it read in the process. If you'd rather clear everything at once without opening each one, there's a single **Mark all as read** action for that. Nothing is ever removed from the list; once a notification arrives, it stays there as either read or unread.

That's the whole feature — see [Viewing and managing notifications](Viewing%20and%20managing%20notifications.md) for the full walkthrough with screenshots.
