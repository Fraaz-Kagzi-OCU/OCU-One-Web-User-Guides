# Collaboration — Overview

**Collaboration** covers the features that let your team discuss and react to records together, and keep track of what's happened to them — comments with threaded replies and internal-only notes, reactions on Hub posts, and each record's Activity feed.

## The three guides

**Commenting on any record, with replies and internal notes** covers the comment thread found on most records — usually labelled "Notes" on screen — including replying to a specific comment and, on a Ticket, marking a note as internal-only. See [[Commenting on any record, with replies and internal notes]].

**Reacting to a comment** covers adding a quick reaction (thumbs-up, applaud, love, laugh, or surprise) to a Hub post, and removing your own reaction again. See [[Reacting to a comment]].

**Viewing a record's activity feed** covers the Activity section found on most records, showing tracked events like creation and status changes with who did them and when. See [[Viewing a record's activity feed]].

## Why this matters

Field-services teams rarely work a record alone — a ticket gets picked up by whoever's free, a job gets signed off by one person and celebrated by the rest of the team, and knowing what changed and when matters for accountability. Comments keep the discussion attached to the record instead of scattered across email or chat, internal notes let a team coordinate without exposing that conversation to a customer, reactions give a lightweight way to acknowledge something without adding to the thread, and the Activity feed gives a quick audit trail of what's actually happened.
