# Invoice Exports — Overview

An **Invoice Export** bundles a batch of invoices together — for example, everything ready to send to a payroll or finance system in one go. It's created from the **Invoices** list by selecting invoices and clicking **Export**, and each invoice included gets its own generated export line (a **bill**) that can be **Valid** or **Invalid**. See [Creating a batch invoice export](Creating%20a%20batch%20invoice%20export.md) for how one is started, [Viewing and managing an invoice export](Viewing%20and%20managing%20an%20invoice%20export.md) for its Overview/Invoices tabs and checking a bill's status, and [Bulk-updating invoice statuses within an export](Bulk-updating%20invoice%20statuses%20within%20an%20export.md) for changing every invoice's status in one action.
